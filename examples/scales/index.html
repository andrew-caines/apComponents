Icons — xs to 2xl, all at the body’s 16px context

Matching names do NOT match sizes — icon-{step} inside text-{step} compounds

Each row sets font-size: text-{step} and the icons to icon-{step}. Because the icon scale is in em, the multiplier applies on top of the row’s own size: icon-2xl (2em) inside text-2xl (32px) renders at 64px. The two only agree at md, where the multiplier is 1.

The pairing that does line up — icon-md inside any text step

icon-md is 1em, so it always equals the surrounding text size. This is the recipe for an icon that sits beside a label.
